
 
# 🌾 KrushiLens

### AI-Powered Crop & Plant Image Analysis Chatbot

KrushiLens is a simple AI-powered vision chatbot that helps users understand what they see in photos of crops, leaves, fruits, and plants.

Users can upload or take a photo and chat with KrushiLens about the visible condition of the plant. The chatbot uses Google's Gemini Vision capabilities to analyze the image and provide practical, easy-to-understand guidance.

---

## 🌱 Why KrushiLens?

Farmers and local growers may notice unusual spots, discoloration, damage, or other visible changes on their plants but may not always know what they indicate.

KrushiLens provides a simple way to:

- 📸 Upload a crop or plant photo
- 🤖 Get AI-based visual analysis
- 💬 Ask follow-up questions about the same image
- 🌿 Understand visible symptoms and possible causes
- ✅ Get practical checks and suggestions
- 📤 Send the crop advice through WhatsApp

> **Note:** KrushiLens is an image-analysis assistant, not a replacement for professional agricultural diagnosis.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📸 Crop & Plant Photo Analysis
Upload an image of a:

- Crop
- Leaf
- Fruit
- Plant
- Agricultural produce

KrushiLens analyzes the visible information in the image.

### 🤖 AI Vision Chat
Gemini Vision is used to understand the uploaded image and answer questions about it.

### 💬 Follow-up Conversation
Users can continue chatting with the AI about the uploaded image instead of starting from the beginning.

### 🌿 Practical Guidance
KrushiLens can provide:

- Visible symptom descriptions
- Possible causes when supported by the image
- Practical checks
- Basic monitoring suggestions

### 📤 WhatsApp Crop Advice
Users can send a summary of their crop-related conversation and advice through WhatsApp.
